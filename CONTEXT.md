# Project glossary

- **Research path**: one user-selected strategy: Fast baseline or Agentic research.
- **Execution mode**: the call/latency budget, Fast or Slow; it is not a separate path.
- **Concept interpretation**: the entities, properties, criteria, scope, and operations needed to answer a question.
- **Concept ambiguity**: multiple plausible interpretations of a question.
- **Disagreement**: divergence between independently generated concept interpretations; a diagnostic signal, not proof of error.
- **Triple quality**: correctness, relevance, and evidence support of subject–predicate–object claims.
